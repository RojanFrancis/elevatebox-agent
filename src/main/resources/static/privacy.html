Privacy policy

ElevateBox voice agent · last updated 31 October 2026

ElevateBox is a demo voice agent for a website-building studio. It calls people who ask for a call on WhatsApp, talks to them about building an e-commerce website, and sends details on WhatsApp. This page says what it keeps and why.

What we store

  • Your WhatsApp number and profile name, when you message our WhatsApp number.
  • When you asked for a call (your consent), and when we last called you, so we never call more than once a day.
  • A short summary of each call, a lead rating (hot, warm or cold) and any callback time you asked for. We do not store call recordings or full transcripts.

How we use it

  • We only call you after you send us a message asking for a call. Calls last at most a few minutes.
  • During or after the call we may send you a WhatsApp message with the details you asked for.
  • We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Services that process it

  • Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, to receive and send WhatsApp messages.
  • Vapi and its telephony provider, to place and run the voice call.
  • Google Gemini, to summarise the call and rate the lead.
  • Render and TiDB Cloud, to host the service and its database.

Deleting your data

To have your number and call history deleted, open a request at github.com/RojanFrancis/elevatebox-agent/issues with the title "Data deletion". Do not post your phone number there: we will reply with a private way to confirm it. We delete your data within 7 days of confirming it.

The source code of this agent is public at github.com/RojanFrancis/elevatebox-agent.
